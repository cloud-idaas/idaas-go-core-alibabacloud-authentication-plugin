# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free community.
Contributors must communicate respectfully, accept constructive feedback, and
focus discussion on the project and its users.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and publication of
another person's private information are unacceptable. Maintainers may edit,
remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation for conduct that violates these expectations.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Reports will
be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.
